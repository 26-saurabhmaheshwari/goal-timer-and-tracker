## Rewards wallet (2026-09-07)
Hours -> spendable money, spent inside a quarter. `LS_REWARDS='ptd_rewards_v1'` `{rate:100, sym:'₹', trate:15, bonus:1.5, tbonus:1.5, lapse:0.5, spends:[], wish:[]}`; SYNC_FIELDS `['rewards', LS_REWARDS, loadRewards, 'map']`. New view `#viewRewards` + nav `data-v="rewards"`; the rate/symbol/trate/bonus/carry controls are a Settings sub-tab (`setRewards`, filled by `renderRewardRules()`, moved out of the Rewards tab 2026-09-07) — the tab shows balances only.
- **Earnings are PURE DERIVED, never stored per entry.** `rewardLedger()` sums `e.mins` per `pkey(e)`, prices each period at `mins/60*rate` and `×bonus` when `periodVerdict()==='hit'`. So editing an old entry, or changing the rate, re-prices the whole history on next paint. Only ONE new key needed. Don't add a per-entry money field.
- **Quarter lifecycle** (the "don't delay enjoying, don't let hard work lapse" rule): money earned in Q is spendable in Q; unspent at Q-end crosses into Q+1 at `lapse` (50%) and the rest lapses; the carried half does NOT roll again. Spending draws from **carried money first** (that is the money about to die). Nothing survives two quarter-ends. `hist` walks quarters from the earliest known to `qOf(CUR_YM)`; the current quarter is `open` (nothing carried/lapsed yet).
- Helpers: `qOf/qNext/qLabel/qEndDate/rwDstr/money`. Spends are `{id,date,amt,note}` and are rejected if the date is outside the current quarter or the amount exceeds `cur.avail`. Wishlist `{id,name,price}` shows "N more periods at your pace" from `perPeriod` (mean over non-`future` periods).
- `#rwWarn` banner is the whole point: red ≤10 days / amber ≤30 when carried money is expiring. `periodEarned()` feeds the period-save flash teaser in `savePeriodEntry`.
- Deliberately NOT built: the optional 20%-of-month "small treat pocket". Rejected framings (user said no): hiding the numbers, streaks/journey metaphors, chance/stakes/story/rival gamification.

### Second currency: earned free time (2026-09-07)
- Logged hours pay **two** balances from the same minutes: `money = hrs*rate` and `free time = hrs*trate` minutes (`trate` default 15, capped 240). Same `bonus` on a green period, same carry/lapse rules, same quarter walk. Free time is stored/computed in MINUTES throughout and only formatted with `fmtShort` at paint.
- The carry/lapse walk was extracted into **`rwWalk(earnQ, spendQ, first, curQ, lapse)`** and is run twice. `rewardLedger()` returns `{R, rows, M, T, curQ, spends, perPeriod, perPeriodT, qEnd, nextQEnd}` — `M` is money, `T` is minutes, each `{hist, cur, lapsedTotal}`. `M.hist` and `T.hist` are index-parallel (same quarter range), which is how the ledger table pairs them. Don't reorder one without the other.
- One spend row can cost both: `{id,date,amt,mins,note}`. `addSpend` needs money OR hours (not both) but validates each against its own `avail` — a spend is rejected if it overdraws either. Wishlist `{id,name,price,mins}`; "how far" takes `Math.max` of the two periods-away numbers, i.e. the **binding** constraint.
- Table cells that show both use `rwBoth()` → money on line 1, `.rw-t` muted line 2 (`+ 3h off`). `rwHrs()` is the minutes formatter wrapper (returns `'0h'` for zero).
- Rules form is now 5 fields, so its inline `repeat(5,1fr)` no longer matches the global mobile `[style*="repeat(4,1fr)"]` override — the Rewards CSS block carries its own `@media(max-width:640px)` collapse for `#rwSpendForm/#rwWishForm/#rwRuleForm .frow`.
- Money and time each have their own green-period multiplier (`bonus` / `tbonus`, both seed 1.5, both clamped 1-5). `rewardLedger` rows carry `mult` and `tmult`; the period table collapses them to one cell when equal. An existing config that had only `bonus` set keeps its money bonus and picks up the 1.5 seed for time.

### Raw entries are no longer purged (2026-09-07)
- `archiveOldEntries()` used to delete raw entries older than 3 months, keeping only monthly snapshots (`MONTHS[].cats` + `LS_ROLLUP`). That silently cost the Rewards wallet its whole pre-window history (it reads `loadEntries()` only) and cost period-granular Reports the same. Fix: **keep the raw entries forever**; the function still writes the snapshots as a backstop but ends with a bare `fbSyncPush()` instead of `saveEntries(filtered)`.
- Size was never the problem the purge solved: an entry is ~120 bytes, localStorage holds ~5MB, so ~40k entries before it matters. Rejected the alternative (make the rollup period-granular) as a bigger schema change for no extra benefit once nothing is deleted.
- Months purged **before** this change are unrecoverable at period level. `rewardLedger()` now folds them in from `loadMonths()` (`total(M.cats)`, the superset of the rollup) as one synthetic row per month, key `'YYYY-MM-P0'`, `verdict:'archived'`, priced at the **base rate with no green bonus** — `periodVerdict()` cannot run without period detail. `arch` rows carry a `label` (`'Mar 2026 (whole month)'`) because `plabel()` can't parse `P0`; the period table reads `r.label||plabel(r.key)`. They are excluded from the per-period averages (`done` filter) since one row is a whole month.
- A ym present in raw entries is never taken from the snapshot (same rule Reports uses). `SEED_MONTHS` is `[]`, so a fresh install earns nothing phantom.

### Period verdict counts the nugget budget (2026-09-07)
- `periodVerdict()` compared logged minutes (habits **and** nugget time) against a plan built from habits only (`perDay × days`). So nugget hours cleared a bar they never raised: ~1h/day of habits made a 10-day period green at 8h even though the nugget budget was another 12h. Fix: `plan = perDay*days + nugBudget*60`.
- Added flat per period, not per day — `nugBudget` (Settings › Limits, default 12) is defined as hours per 10-day period. So a 10-day period targets 22h and an 11-day P3 targets 23h at 60 min/day of habits.
- Read via `loadLimits().nugBudget`, **not** the `NUGGET_BUDGET` module var: that var is `let`-declared ~1300 lines below `periodVerdict` (TDZ risk if anything ever calls the verdict at load time), and `saveLimits()` only refreshes the var.
- The nugget term must live inside `periodVerdict`, not be folded into the `perDay` argument by callers — `rewardLedger()` and the 36-cell poster loop pass `perDay` in as an optimisation.
- Behaviour change, not a display fix: every verdict everywhere gets stricter (Rewards period table + quarter earnings, Overview, period modal, witness notes, journey poster). It retro-prices the whole wallet — six of the user's eight green periods dropped, ~a third of the shown money and free time. Historical nugget targets were never recorded, so the current flat budget stands in for every past period.
- Per-habit streak math at `HABIT_HIT`/`habitStreak` still uses that habit's own plan only — correct, left alone.

### Rewards KPIs became two balance blocks; verdict legend; pot popup dropped (2026-09-07)
- The four KPI cards (Available now / Earned / Used / Carried in) each crammed money on the big line and free time into the small line, so neither balance was readable. Now `#rwKpis` is a 2-column grid of one block per currency (`.rwbal`): headline = that currency's `avail`, then a 3-row table (carried in / earned / used), then its own rate + green bonus. `balBlock(label, head, fmt, c, foot)` takes the currency's formatter (`money` or `rwHrs`) so one function serves both. Stacks to one column under 640px — the inner tables need the width.
- The "expires <date>" note on the Carried in row is per-currency (`c.expSoon>=1`), not the shared `soon` flag: money can be spent out while free time still expires.
- `#rwLegend` under the period table states the bar in hours, computed live from habits + `nugBudget`: plan, then the 80%/40% cut-offs. Quoted for a flat 10-day period even though P3 scores 1-2 days longer — the round number is what the user reasons with, and the real scoring stays per-period.
- Removed the pot popup that auto-opened once per 10-day period (markup, `showPotPopup`/`closePotPopup`, the trigger IIFE, and its `body.pdf-print` rule). The pot still lives in Overview > Pot, opened deliberately. The `potPopupShown` localStorage key is orphaned and harmless (it was never in `SYNC_FIELDS`).

### Quarter-ledger column tints + legend trim (2026-09-07)
- `#rwLegend` lost its closing "Change either half in Settings…" sentence — the legend is read every period and the pointer only mattered once.
- Quarter ledger tints only the four columns that carry a judgement: Earned green `#2f6b46`, Used `var(--accent)` (spending it is the point), Rolled on amber `#8a6a17` (survived but expires next quarter), Lapsed red `#9c2b1c`. ### Reports export became a one-month hard copy (2026-09-07)
- Still ONE export button (`#repPdfBtn`, now just "⤓ Export"), so the three-export-surface rule holds. A `<select id="repMonth">` beside it picks the month; its last option `__year` still routes to the old `exportReportPdf()` year-wide chart dump, so nothing was lost and `pdfClone`/`pdfSec`/`pdfSub` stay in use.
- `monthReportHTML(ym)` is purpose-written, not a clone of rendered views — a year dump is a data dump, one month fits on paper. Order is deliberate: three headline figures (hours, money earned, free time earned) with the % delta vs last month and this month's rank among all logged months → the three period verdicts against plan → category split → habits kept → goals that moved → top 10 tasks → a page break, then ruled blank lines for "proud of / fixing next month / aiming for" plus sign-and-date. The pen part is the reason to print at all.
- Sources, so there is one truth: `rewardLedger().rows` filtered to the `ym` for verdict/multiplier/pay, `entryCats(ym)` for the split, `type==='project' && task===h.n` for habit minutes with `HABIT_HIT` for the per-period ✓, `goal.logs[]` rows filtered `!l.via` for goal amounts (mini echo rows double-count otherwise).
- `monthMins(ym)` prefers live entries and falls back to a `loadMonths()` category snapshot, so pre-rollup months still export (headline hours only — no period or task detail exists for them).
- No day-of-month on entries, so there is deliberately no "best day" anywhere in the sheet.
- Print CSS is `#pdfReport .mr-*` — flat ink on white, `.mr-bar` as a 7px div (no canvas), `.mr-brk{break-before:page}` for the hand-written page.
- Month report v2 (same day): "Goals that moved" rolls minis up into the parent — a mini is never shown by its own name anywhere in the app (it renders as "N units closer to <parent>"), so rows read "mini +80 Minutes" on paper. Folding is safe against double-count because parent echo rows carry `via` and are already filtered; the row shows the parent's name, unit, cur/target and a "· N steps" note. Added four more sections: a 12-month bar list with one 🟢 per green period, the green-period streak in the headline note, "What you cashed in" (this month's spends, with a deliberately blunt line when nothing was booked — earning and never spending is the failure mode the wallet exists to stop), the carried-in expiry date and amount, the cheapest wishlist item you cannot afford yet with periods-to-go at your own pace, and next month's ask in one number (hours needed for three green periods, and per day).

### Four verdict bands + a bonus ladder (2026-09-07)
- `periodVerdict` now returns four scored values instead of three: `hit` >=100%, `partial` (amber) 75-99%, `low` (orange) 40-74%, `miss` (red) <40%. `future`/`none`/`archived` unchanged. Green moved from 80% to 100% at the user's request — the bar is now "do the plan", not "do most of it".
- Bonus is a ladder, not a cliff. New `verdMult(v, b)` right below `periodVerdict`: green pays the full bonus, amber pays `1 + (bonus-1)/2` (half the bonus, so 1.5× becomes 1.25×), orange and red pay 1×. It is derived from the user's own bonus setting deliberately — no second number to configure and no new sync field. Both multiplier sites (`rewardLedger` and `periodEarned`) call it, so they cannot drift apart.
- Red still pays the base rate. Those hours were real work; docking them would punish showing up. The only thing lost below amber is the bonus.
- Reason for the ladder: at three bands, 79% and 5% paid identically, so a near-miss felt like a wasted period. Partial credit makes 65%→75% worth pushing for.
- Orange is `#e2711d` everywhere (wall poster cells + legend, stones jar), picked to stay distinct from amber `#f59e0b` in print.
- Consumers touched, all of which needed the 4th key: the period-save flash `VW`, `periodWitness`'s `V`, `printYearPoster`'s `VC` + its swatch legend, the stones jar `VC` and its `nHit/nPart/nLow/nMiss` tally line, `RW_VERD`, `#rwLegend` (now quotes all four cut-offs in hours plus which pay a bonus), and two month-report notes (the plan note and the next-month ask, which used to hardcode `nxPlan*0.8`).
- `HABIT_HIT=0.8` is untouched — it scores one habit against its own plan, not a period verdict. Don't conflate the two.
- Caveat stated to the user: with a 22h period plan their 8 logged periods averaged 11.8h (54%), which is orange. A 100% green bar only makes sense after the plan comes down to ~12h (25 min/day habits + 8h nugget budget). Thresholds changed; the plan is still theirs to set in Settings.
- Removed `#pgPdfBtn`, the duplicate "⤓ Export" in the page header that proxied a click to `#repPdfBtn` and sat left of the sign-in email. Reports still has its own button, so the three-export-surface rule is unaffected (this was a fourth surface in all but name).

### The 10-day paper sheet, rebuilt (2026-09-08)
- Context: the user logs on paper for nine days and types totals into the period modal on the tenth. They do not want to open the app daily, so a screen-based daily checklist was rejected. The paper IS the daily driver — it has to carry the plan, the order and the targets on its own.
- `printPeriodSheet()` replaced wholesale. Same button (`#pePrint`, now labelled "10-day sheet"), same surface, no new export surface.
- Order enforcement is typographic, not code: habits print above a 3px black rule reading "Habits first — nothing below until the block above is filled", nuggets below it. The app cannot verify daily behaviour (entries carry no day-of-month), so the rule on the page is the whole mechanism. Don't try to enforce it in JS.
- Period puzzle: one 13px square per 30 minutes of the period plan (`perDay*days + nugBudget*60`), squares already earned pre-shaded from logged minutes at print time. The amber (75%) and orange (40%) cut-offs are marked as coloured left edges inside the grid, so the paper shows where you stand without arithmetic. A full grid = green.
- Per-nugget puzzle bars: boxes toward `g.target`, `g.cur` pre-shaded. Targets over 30 fold several units into one box (`per = ceil(target/30)`) and print "1 box = N unit" rather than 400 squares.
- Day grid gains a weekday initial under each day number and a `started at` row (the user has a physical timer — the app never runs a stopwatch, the sheet just captures the clock time).
- Row order matches the period modal's row order so the last-day transcription is one pass down the Total column.
- A4 landscape, `@page{size:A4 landscape;margin:0}`. Palette is the app's own (`#3d5266` accent, `#f6f2e8` header bands, `#8d8676` muted) plus the four verdict colours in the band legend only.
- Rejected: a daily "Today" tick strip in the app with nuggets locked until habits are ticked. Correct mechanic, wrong medium — it needs a daily app visit.
